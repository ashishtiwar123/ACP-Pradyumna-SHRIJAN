EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
PayPulse AI is building the next-generation autonomous fraud detection and payment intelligence platform for mid-market e-commerce merchants and financial institutions. By combining real-time transaction telemetry with graph neural networks, PayPulse reduces false decline rates by 45% while blocking sophisticated fraud attempts, saving merchants millions in lost revenues annually.

THE PROBLEM
-----------
1. Legacy Fraud Prevention is Rigid & Slow: Traditional rule-based engines miss new fraud patterns, causing up to $40B annually in chargebacks globally.
2. High False Decline Rates: Up to 15% of legitimate customer transactions are falsely rejected by outdated anti-fraud rules, alienating high-value buyers.
3. Resource-Intensive Operations: Mid-market fintechs spend over 20% of their operational budget on manual risk review teams.

THE SOLUTION
------------
PayPulse AI provides an end-to-end payment intelligence API that integrates in under 15 minutes:
- Predictive Behavioral AI: Evaluates 250+ device, network, and biometric signals per transaction.
- Instant Decisioning Engine: Delivers risk decisions in under 45 milliseconds.
- Dynamic 3D Secure Verification: Triggers friction-free authentication only when high risk is detected.
