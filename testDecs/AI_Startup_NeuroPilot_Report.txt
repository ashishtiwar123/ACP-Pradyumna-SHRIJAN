--------------------------------------------------
1. STARTUP OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------

NeuroPilot AI is an enterprise artificial intelligence platform that helps mid-sized and large businesses automate internal knowledge discovery and employee workflows.

The platform connects with company knowledge sources such as documents, internal databases, project-management tools, and communication systems. Employees can ask questions in natural language and receive context-aware answers, summaries, and workflow recommendations.

The company's primary value proposition is reducing the time employees spend searching for information and performing repetitive knowledge-based tasks.

Business Model:
B2B SaaS subscription model.

Target Customers:
- Mid-sized technology companies
- Financial services companies
- Consulting firms
- Enterprise operations teams

Primary Market:
India initially, with expansion potential into Southeast Asia and other international markets.
